# DataPulse - ETL,Data,Modeling and Business Analytics
An Excel-based Business Intelligence project focused on sales performance analysis, financial reporting, ETL, data modeling, and KPI tracking using Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX to transform raw data into actionable business insights.

Sales & Finance Analytics

📌 About the Project

Analyzed sales and financial data using Excel to understand business performance, track sales targets, and identify growth opportunities.

📈 Sales Report

Created a Customer Performance Report.

Analyzed market performance against sales targets.

Identified sales trends and customer growth opportunities.

💰 Finance Report

Created P&L reports by Fiscal Year, Month, and Market.

Tracked profitability and compared financial performance.

🛠️ Tools & Skills

Microsoft Excel

Power Query & Power Pivot

DAX and Data Modeling

ETL and Fiscal Calendar Creation

🎯 Key Takeaway

Gained hands-on experience in data cleaning, analysis, reporting, and transforming raw data into meaningful business insights.
